# TypeWhisper

## 1.0.0

First release.

- TypeWhisper dictation opens the chat by itself and keeps an open chat with what you already typed.
- A cast bar in the game's own style shows the dictation: a waveform while you speak, a filling bar while TypeWhisper transcribes, and a red bar when a dictation is canceled.
- Settings in the game, under Options › AddOns › TypeWhisper or with `/tw`: send messages automatically, show the cast bar, translate your messages.
- Commands `/tw test`, `/tw move`, `/tw scale` and `/tw reset` for the cast bar.
